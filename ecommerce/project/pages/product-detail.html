Premium Quality Denim

Our premium denim jeans are meticulously crafted from high-quality cotton with just the right amount of stretch for all-day comfort without losing their shape. The classic five-pocket design and versatile slim fit make these jeans a wardrobe staple that transitions seamlessly from day to night.

Features

  • 98% cotton, 2% elastane for comfort and flexibility
  • Slim fit through hip and thigh with a slightly tapered leg
  • Mid-rise waist with belt loops
  • Classic five-pocket styling
  • Button closure and zip fly
  • Signature stitching on back pockets
  • Pre-washed for a soft hand feel
  • Sustainably produced using eco-friendly dyes

Care Instructions

Machine wash cold with similar colors. Tumble dry low. Do not bleach. Warm iron if needed.
